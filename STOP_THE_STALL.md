# Coastline – Stop The Stall (keyword → close, no call-backs)

© 2026 Morgan / Coastline Auto Detailing. All rights reserved. Confidential — do not copy or share without written permission.

**The rule:** every call/text ends one of two ways — **booked**, or **a spot held with a YES-by-tonight deadline.** Never "I'll follow up" or "let me check back." If you catch yourself typing/saying "follow up next week," stop — you skipped the deadline line.

## Trigger → one-liner (say this, then shut up)

| They say | Say this |
| --- | --- |
| "Let me talk to my wife/husband." | "Makes sense — is she around? I can answer anything real quick. Either way, I'll text you the price and pics now and hold [day] until 8pm tonight. Reply YES." |
| "Give me a minute / give me a second." | "Take your time — what's the one thing you're deciding on? I'll hold [day] until tonight either way." |
| "I'll call you back." | "For sure — real quick, what do you still need to know? [Answer it.] Since we're already talking, let's just grab [day] at [time] or [day] at [time]." |
| "Let me think about it." | "Of course — usually there's one thing. What's the one thing? [Fix it.] If we take care of that, good to book [day]?" |
| "Let me check my schedule." | "What's your week look like, mornings or afternoons? I'll pencil [day] and you can move it if needed." |
| "Just getting prices." | "Smart — besides price, what matters most: quality, us coming to you, or speed? [Answer.] I can get you in [day]. Want it?" |
| "That's more than I wanted to spend." | Never lower the price. "What were you hoping to get done? For $[lower] we can do just the interior/exterior." |
| "Not right now, maybe next month." | "Is it timing or budget? Let's lock [date next month] now so you keep a good spot." |
| "I'll book online later." | "Takes 30 seconds — let me just do it now. What's your address?" |

## The closer's closer (use every single time, no exceptions)

If they still won't book after the above:

> "No problem — I'll hold [day] for you until [time] tonight. Just reply YES and you're locked in."

Then **stop talking.** Don't soften it, don't re-open negotiation. If no YES by the deadline, they didn't want it — that's fine, move to the next lead. That's what closes the loop instead of an endless follow-up chain.

## Never do this

- Never say "I'll follow up with you" as the last line.
- Never lower the price — change the service instead (smaller package, different day/location).
- Never leave a call without either a booking or a same-night deadline.
