Target: open a second mobile detailing location serving Glen Mills, PA (19342) and the surrounding Delaware County / Chester County area in **March 2028**, run the same way as Athens: Morgan runs the business, local techs and subcontractors do the work, and leads come from Google Local Services Ads first, then Meta ads for ceramic coating.

## Finding techs

Local pay benchmarks: dealership detailer jobs in Glen Mills pay about **$15–20/hour** (CarShop Glen Mills $15–17; Concordville Nissan $18–20). A per-job rate should beat that to attract good people. For example, at Athens rates a tech doing 3 full details a day earns $255.

Where to recruit:
- Indeed and ZipRecruiter "car detailer" posts for Glen Mills / Delaware County / West Chester
- Detailers already working at local dealerships and car washes along Baltimore Pike (Route 1) who want more money or side work
- Local Facebook groups, detailing groups, and community college auto programs (Delaware County Community College)
- Referrals from your Athens techs

What to offer: per-job pay (not hourly), paid training, and a path to lead tech. Run a paid test detail before hiring.
