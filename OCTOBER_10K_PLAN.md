**Goal:** $10,000 revenue in October. November stays at $20,000.

## 1. The math

| Number | Target |
| --- | --- |
| Revenue | $10,000 |
| Jobs | About 45 (average ticket $225) |
| Per week | About 11 jobs / $2,250–$3,000 |
| Per day | About 1–2 jobs / $325 |
| Leads needed | About 113 (at a 40% close rate), so about 4 a day |
| Google reviews | 31 (1 a day) |
| Net profit | About $5,000 after tech pay, Rapid Reach, ads and supplies |

Your 5 techs can easily handle 45 jobs. **Leads and closing are the limit**, so that's where your time goes.

## 2. Every day (non-negotiable)

1. **Reply to every lead in under 5 minutes.** Text first, then call. Leads contacted within 5 minutes close about 2.6× more often than leads contacted a day later.
2. **Check GoHighLevel 3 times:** late morning, mid-afternoon, evening.
3. **Follow up** every lead that didn't book: day 1, day 3, day 7.
4. **Get 1 Google review.** Text the review link within an hour of every finished job.
5. **Post 1 before-and-after** on Instagram, TikTok and Facebook.
6. **Offer 1 add-on or upgrade on every job** (interior → full for $50 more, pet hair, odor).
7. **Log every job and expense in Jarvis**, and split every paid invoice the same day (25% taxes, 30% business costs, 15% savings, 30% your pay).

## 4. Quick wins that close more deals

- **Answer after-hours leads automatically.** 40–50% of leads come in outside business hours. A GoHighLevel auto-text ("Got your request! What's the year, make and model?") keeps them warm until you call.
- **Give 3 options, not 1.** Exterior $170 / Full $250 / a premium package. Most people pick the middle one.
- **Never discount. Downsell instead** (interior $200 or exterior $170).
- **Always give 2 time options and a deadline** ("I'll hold it until 8pm tonight").
- **Use the Closing Scripts sheet** for every objection ("I'll hold off," "I need to talk to my wife," "too expensive").

## 5. If you're behind by the 15th

- Text every past customer and every lead from the last 60 days with one limited offer (a free add-on, not a lower price).
- Post a customer testimonial video.
- Ask every happy customer for a referral ($25 off for both).
- Pitch 3 local businesses with company vehicles.
