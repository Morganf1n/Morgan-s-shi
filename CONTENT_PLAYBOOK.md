# Coastline content playbook

What's working for detailing videos on TikTok, Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts (September 2026), and how to use it without copying anyone.

## What the top detailing videos have in common

- **The payoff comes first.** The best-performing clips show the reveal, the foam hitting the paint, or the worst of the mess in the first second. About 65% of viewers leave within 3 seconds, so no logo intros and no "hey guys."
- **Transformation beats explanation.** Filthy interior → spotless, swirls disappearing under a polisher, and snow foam get millions of views because nobody needs detailing knowledge to enjoy them.
- **Short and rewatchable.** Transformations under 60 seconds get about 70% completion. Tutorials can run up to about 90 seconds.
- **Sound matters.** Extractor slurps, pressure washer hiss, foam cannon and quiet ASMR audio outperform generic music.
- **Consistency beats skill.** Accounts that grow post 3–5 times a week. Best times: Tue, Thu and Sat evenings. Post before/afters Friday through Saturday morning, when people are thinking about their car for the weekend.

## Niches to own (pick 1–2 and repeat them)

| Niche | Why it works for Coastline |
| --- | --- |
| **"Worst car of the week"** (dog hair, smoker, kid-trashed minivan, sand) | The most-watched detailing format on every platform. It's also a paid upsell: detailers charge 15–25% extra for pet hair and smoke. |
| **Coastline / beach cars** (sand, salt, sunscreen stains) | This is your brand name, and hardly anyone owns it. Salt and sand are local problems your viewers actually have. |
| **The owner's side: running 5 techs at 18** | Almost no detailing accounts show the business side. "I don't detail cars, I run the company" is a hook by itself and attracts customers, future techs and business followers. |
| **"Stuff that's ruining your paint"** (bird droppings, sap, water spots, automatic car washes) | Creates mild worry, then positions you as the fix. Great for getting booking DMs. |
| **Customer reveal reactions** | Doubles as testimonial content (see below). |

**Recommendation:** lead with **"Worst car of the week"** on TikTok and Reels and **"18 and running 5 techs"** as your personal-brand series. The first brings views and bookings. The second makes you memorable.

## Hooks to test (write your own versions)

Put the words on screen and say them in the first second, over the most dramatic shot.

Mess hooks
- "This is the worst car we've seen all year." (open on the worst spot)
- "The customer said 'don't judge me'… we judged."
- "How much dog hair can one Tahoe hold?"
- "She said her kids eat in here. We believe her."

Reveal / result hooks
- Start on the finished car, then: "This was the same car 4 hours ago."
- "Watch the swirls disappear." (polisher on paint, real time)
- "$250 vs. what it looked like before."

Paint-worry hooks
- "If your car has these spots, stop washing it like this."
- "The #1 thing ruining your paint in [your city]." (salt / sun / sprinklers)

Owner / business hooks
- "I'm 18 and I have 5 detailing technicians. Here's how."
- "I didn't touch a single car today and we made $X." (only use your real numbers)
- "What I pay my techs vs. what I charge." ($250 full / $85 tech: people love transparency)

## Testimonials your techs can film

Keep them 30–60 seconds, vertical, and filmed at the reveal, while the reaction is real.

1. **The walk-around reveal.** The tech opens the door, the customer sees the interior for the first time, and you film their face and the car. Don't coach them. The raw "oh my god" is the ad.
2. **Three quick questions** (tech asks off-camera):
   - "What did it look like before?"
   - "What made you book Coastline?"
   - "Would you tell a friend?"
3. **Before photo + review screenshot.** Put the customer's Google review over the before/after. It doubles as a review request reminder.
4. **"Tag your messiest friend."** End with the customer saying who else needs this. That's free referrals.

Always get a quick "is it okay if we post this?" on camera or by text.

## A simple weekly plan (3–5 posts)

| Day | Post |
| --- | --- |
| Tue | Worst car of the week: full transformation (under 60s) |
| Thu | Paint-worry tip or swirl-removal clip |
| Sat | Customer reveal testimonial |
| + 1–2 | Owner series: numbers, running techs, a day in your life |

Track in Jarvis: log which hook you used as a Today goal, and compare views after 48 hours. Keep what wins and drop what doesn't.
