## How to get 9–10 a week

1. Ask in person on every job and text the link while the customer is at the car.
2. Text 10 past customers a week who haven't reviewed.
3. GoHighLevel: review link 1 hour after every job, reminder 2 days later.
4. $5 bonus to Austin and Mo for each review that names them.
5. Reply to every review within 24 hours.

## Rules

- Never pay customers or give discounts for reviews.
- No fake reviews.
- Ask every customer, not just the happy ones.
