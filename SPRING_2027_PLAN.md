**Spring target:** $135,000 in revenue from March to May 2027 at a 50%+ profit margin, about $67,500 net.

Spring is detailing's busy season in Athens. Pollen starts in late February and peaks from late March to mid-April, so every car in town is yellow. The weather is also perfect for ceramic coatings, which need 50–60°F or warmer. Put coatings and maintenance plans front and center.

## 2. Team

- **About 140 jobs a month** means 5–6 jobs a day, plus coatings that can take a full day each.
- **You'll need 8–10 techs by May.** Hire 3–5 more in January and February so they're trained before pollen hits.
- **Name a lead tech** for quality checks and coating jobs.
- **Consider a part-time dispatcher or VA** to answer leads in under 5 minutes while you sell and manage.

## 4. Marketing plan for spring

- **Meta ads (main engine):** coating and pollen ads. Before-and-afters, water beading, "pollen slides right off." $50–100/day by March if cost per booked coating stays under $150.
- **Google Local Services Ads + Google Search:** "car detailing Athens GA," "ceramic coating Athens," "pollen car wash." Raise the budget as long as each booked job pays.
- **Google Business Profile:** a post every week (pollen tips, offers, before-and-afters). Reply to every review.
- **Content:** 1 video a day. Spring hooks: "This is what pollen does to your paint," "Yellow car? It's not supposed to be," "Watch the pollen slide off a coated car."
- **Maintenance plan push:** offer it to every spring customer. Goal: 60 members by May 31.
- **Fleet:** pitch dealerships (spring is car-buying season), landscapers, property managers and realtors. Goal: 3 accounts by May 31.
- **Referrals:** $25 off for both people. Ask every happy customer.
- **Past customers:** text everyone from 2026 in February with the Pollen Protection offer.
