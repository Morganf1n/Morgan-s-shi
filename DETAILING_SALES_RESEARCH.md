What's working in the detailing industry right now (research, September 2026), and what Coastline should do with it.

## 1. Closing more deals

### Speed to lead is the #1 lever

| Finding | Source |
| --- | --- |
| Leads contacted within 5 minutes closed at **32%**, vs **12%** for leads contacted 24+ hours later (2.6×). | 2026 study of 939 companies (Rework) |
| Contact within 60 seconds: **391% higher** close rate. | Velocify, 3.5 million leads |
| Average business response time is **47 minutes**; only ~0.1% reply within 5 minutes. | Ringlead / Rework |
| **40–50% of leads arrive outside business hours**, and those get the slowest replies. | Ringlead |

**What Coastline should do:**

- Instant GoHighLevel text on every new lead, 24/7, plus missed-call text-back.
- Call within 5 minutes during the day. Call after-hours leads first thing the next morning.
- Instant online booking cuts out back-and-forth that lets leads go cold.
- Motivated buyers (selling a car, a wedding or event) book fast and rarely shop around once they trust you. Ask "What's the occasion?" and use the date to close.

### Handling objections

- **Sell value, don't defend the price.** Explain the products, the training and the results.
- **Downsell, don't discount.** Offer a smaller package to budget-conscious customers.
- **Mobile is worth more.** Mobile detailers can charge a $25–50 convenience premium over a shop, because you save the customer 1–2 hours.
- Full scripts for 18 objections are in the **Coastline Closing Scripts** sheet.

### Selling ceramic coatings

- **Frame it as a loss happening now.** People act on what they're losing today more than what they might gain later. Show what's happening to their unprotected paint right now: swirls, fading, water spots.
- **Show all the tiers on one quote** (for example 1-year, 3-year, 5-year). The customer upsells themselves.
- **Before-and-after pictures close premium jobs** much better than a text-only quote.
- **Every detail customer is a coating prospect.** Pitch it at the end of every job.

## 2. Price management

### Use Good / Better / Best packages

- Tiered packages can lead to about **32% more sales** (Jobber).
- **Most customers choose the middle option**, because the top tier makes the middle feel reasonable (anchoring).
- **2026 mobile detailing averages** (89 operators in 4 big metros): basic **$158**, standard **$278**, premium **$443**. Interior work (shampoo, steam, leather) drives the jump between tiers.

**Coastline example lineup** (set your own tech pay for the new tier):

| Tier | Price | What's in it | Tech pay | Rapid Reach | You keep |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Good: Exterior wash | $170 | Exterior | $40 | $25 | $105 (62%) |
| Better: Full detail | $250 | Interior + exterior | $85 | $25 | $140 (56%) |
| Best: Signature detail | $349 (example) | Full + pet hair, stain extraction, odor treatment, leather conditioning, paint sealant | Set it (e.g. $110) | $25 | ~$214 (61%) |

A $349 "best" option makes $250 look like the smart choice. Some customers will pick $349 anyway.

### When and how to raise prices

- Raise prices **5–10% a year**. Products, gas and insurance cost more every year, and your work gets better.
- Give customers **30 days' notice** and frame it as better service and rising costs.
- Big metro averages are above Athens prices, so check local competitors before raising. Your $250 full detail is below the $278 national "standard" average, so there's room over time.
- Keep every service at **50%+ margin** after tech pay and Rapid Reach.

## 3. Marketing

### Google Business Profile and reviews

- Your Google Business Profile is **the most valuable marketing you own**.
- Get a review after every job. **Reply to every review, good or bad.**
- Post updates and offers every week. Google ranks active profiles higher in the local map pack.

### Meta (Facebook + Instagram) ads

- **What works:** before-and-afters, coating application videos, water-beading clips, gloss close-ups.
- **Hook in the first 3 seconds** of every video.
- **Static images still drive about 60–70% of Meta conversions.** Carousels are the top format in 2026. Test all three.
- **Use Lead Form ads to start.** Name and phone fill in from Facebook, and it takes about 10 seconds to submit.
- **Lead with a guarantee or warranty** in the ad. One shop's best ad led with its warranty.
- **Add 1 new creative a week** so ads don't go stale.

### Repeat customers and recurring revenue

- Most detailers treat a finished job as the end. **The businesses that stay booked treat it as the start.**
- **Maintenance plan:** a monthly or every-6-weeks maintenance detail turns one-time customers into steady income. (Vendors claim 40 members can bring in $7k+ a month; results vary.)
- **Reminder texts:** text customers when their next detail is due (for example 60–90 days, or 6 months for coating check-ups). These bookings cost nothing to get.
- **Seasonal offers** (fall, holidays, pollen season) bring past customers back.
